On writing well in a hurry

John Gruber · May 23, 2026 · open original

Good prose is clear before it is clever. Delete the middle of every sentence and see if it still stands.

If a reader's eye drifts mid-paragraph, it isn't the reader's fault — the paragraph asked too much.
